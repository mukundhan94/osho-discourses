# Product

Osho discourse chronology: a single self-contained HTML reference page listing Osho's talks in date order, by year, with series, dates, location and talk count. Shared as a secret gist.

## Platform
web

## Stack
One static HTML file, inline CSS and minimal inline JS, no build step, no external requests. Rendered through htmlpreview from a gist, and opened locally.

## Users
Friends of the author who already know Osho and his talks (confirmed by the author, 2026-11-16). They know the names of the well-known series and want to place them in time and place. Not newcomers: no need to explain who Osho was.

## Product Purpose
Let a reader go year by year through the talks and see what was spoken, when, where, and how many talks (confirmed main job: read year by year). Currently covers 1954 to 1967; the research continues through 1990 and the page grows by appending years.

## Positioning
A compact, honest digest of the Sannyas Wiki year timelines, which are exhaustive but hundreds of rows per year.

## Operating Context
Read on a laptop or phone, at leisure, usually from a shared link.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Data source: Sannyas Wiki "Osho Timeline" year pages, plus Osho Source Book and Neeten's Ranakpur paper for the first camp.
- Talk counts are rows on the wiki, not verified totals.
- Which talks have surviving audio was not checked; the page must say so.
- Many titles are later book compilations of scattered talks.
- All talks in this period are in Hindi, in India.

## Brand Commitments
None. No ashram, foundation or publisher branding; this is an independent reference. No em-dashes in copy.

## Evidence on Hand
The per-year series tables gathered on 2026-11-16 (in the session), with source URLs.

## Product Principles
- Uncertainty stays attached to the row it belongs to.
- Chronology is the spine; nothing reorders it.
- Sources are always one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard reachable, readable at phone width, respects reduced motion. Hindi titles are romanised as on the wiki.
